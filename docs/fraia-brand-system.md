# Fraia Brand System

This document is the canonical source for Fraia's public brand identity. It does not change the product interface theme or engineering visualisation colours.

## Brand idea

Fraia helps engineers turn structural intent into models that can be analysed, compared, and traced. The identity should feel precise and capable without copying the cold blue language common to engineering software.

The symbol combines three ideas:

- an abstract `F` silhouette;
- an anchored cantilever and vault;
- a circular analysis boundary passing through a structural frame.

The symbol must remain a single, legible glyph. Do not add letters, gradients, shadows, background tiles, or internal decoration.

## Primary palette

| Name | Value | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Carbon | `#24211F` | Stable mark, primary text, dark neutral |
| Chalk | `#F5F1EB` | Stable mark on dark surfaces, warm light surface |
| Oxide | `#B95232` | Beta mark, warm emphasis, release-channel identity |
| Oxide Light | `#EF795F` | Beta mark and emphasis on dark surfaces |
| Load Amber | `#D6A24A` | Supporting marketing accent |
| Patina | `#667462` | Supporting natural accent |
| Stone | `#837A70` | Supporting neutral |

Carbon and Chalk define the stable identity. Oxide and Oxide Light define the beta identity. Beta is part of the Fraia palette, not a separate purple or blue brand.

## Icon use

- Stable light appearance: Carbon glyph on the native surface.
- Stable dark appearance: Chalk glyph on the native surface.
- Beta light appearance: Oxide glyph on the native surface.
- Beta dark appearance: Oxide Light glyph on the native surface.
- Keep the background transparent. Let macOS or the host surface provide its native background.
- Preserve the source view box and clear space. Do not stretch, rotate, outline, or place the mark in a fixed coloured square.
- At small sizes, use the generated release assets. Do not redraw the geometry for each platform.

## Voice

Use direct language. Describe an engineering action and its result. Prefer specific terms such as model, support, load, analysis run, option, and provenance. Avoid claims that Fraia replaces engineering judgement or performs final code-compliant design.

Primary description:

> Fraia turns structural intent into traceable models, preliminary analysis, and design options.

Early-stage notice:

> Fraia is early-stage software for preliminary work. It does not replace project-specific engineering judgement or code-compliant design.
